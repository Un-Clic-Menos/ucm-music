# UCM Music

**Find, preview and download music. All in one place.**

UCM Music is a Windows desktop application designed to make finding and downloading music simple. Search for songs using Deezer, preview tracks before downloading, or paste links from YouTube, YouTube Music and Spotify to find the music you want.

Whether you're looking for a single track or an entire playlist, UCM Music brings everything together in one straightforward interface.

🌐 **Official website:** https://unclicmenos.com

---

## Features

### 🔎 Music Search

Search for songs by name using the Deezer API.

- Search tracks by title or artist.
- Browse matching search results.
- Find the song you're looking for without needing a direct URL.

### 🎧 30-Second Track Previews

Listen before downloading.

- Preview up to 30 seconds of a song.
- Previews are provided through the Deezer API.
- Check that you've found the correct track before proceeding.

### 🔗 Link-Based Search

Can't find the song through the search bar? Simply paste a link.

Supported sources:

- YouTube
- YouTube Music
- Spotify

UCM Music uses the provided link to locate the corresponding music. For Spotify links, it searches for the matching track on YouTube.

### 📃 Playlist Support

Download entire playlists instead of searching for every song individually.

- YouTube playlists.
- Spotify playlists.

For Spotify playlists, UCM Music searches for each track on YouTube to locate the corresponding audio.

### 🎼 Multiple Audio Formats

Choose your preferred output format before downloading:

- MP3
- FLAC
- WAV

### 📁 Custom Output Folder

Choose where your downloads are saved.

- Select your preferred destination folder.
- Downloads are automatically saved to the selected location.

---

## How It Works

1. **Search or paste a link:** Find music by name or use a YouTube, YouTube Music or Spotify URL.
2. **Preview (optional):** Listen to a 30-second preview when searching through Deezer.
3. **Choose your format:** Select MP3, FLAC or WAV.
4. **Select your output folder:** Choose where the downloaded files will be saved.
5. **Download:** Press the Download button and let UCM Music handle the process.
